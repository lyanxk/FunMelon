Additional cultivar artwork (yellow, unripe, glistering, enchanted, ender)

Geometry: the same Tea the Story / MalayP five fruit poses and leaf model,
under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 as documented above. Fruit face UVs now span the
complete 16x16 source texture; pose geometry and rotations are retained.
Cultivar leaf PNGs are recolored adaptations of the Tea grayscale leaf
texture, edited in Blockbench while retaining its four shading levels,
alpha silhouette, geometry and UVs. Fixed colors are yellow (yellow),
pale mint (unripe), gold-orange (glistering), cyan (enchanted), and
purple-pink (ender). Cultivar leaf face tint indices are removed; the
ordinary melon keeps its original grayscale texture and tintindex=0.
These Tea adaptations retain CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
